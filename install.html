Add Guardians of the Ocean to your phone as an app. This page installs it — it does not open the browser version.

Preparing install…
iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — then open the app icon, not Safari.
Chrome or Edge on Android can install in one tap. Safari needs Add to Home Screen.
